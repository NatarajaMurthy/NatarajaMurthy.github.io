1. Introduction to MoneyFlowTracker

MoneyFlowTracker is an offline-first, privacy-respecting financial operating system for Android. Unlike conventional expense trackers that sell your data or require sensitive bank login credentials, MoneyFlowTracker keeps your financial data encrypted on your device with military-grade 256-bit AES encryption.

Core Highlights

  • 100% Privacy & Local Security: Your records remain on your phone unless you explicitly choose to back them up to your private Google Drive or sync via encrypted cloud.
  • Hands-Free Detection: Detects incoming bank SMS alerts and notifications to record expenses with a single tap.
  • Smart AI Companion: Built-in Google Gemini financial assistant analyzes your habits and gives personalized tips.
  • Comprehensive Wealth Overview: One consolidated dashboard for Cash, Bank Accounts, Credit Cards, Debts, and Investments.

2. Getting Started & Initial Setup

When you open MoneyFlowTracker for the first time, a streamlined setup wizard guides you through essential configurations:

Step 1: Choose Your Base Currency

  1. Select your local currency from the international currency list (e.g., USD , EUR €, GBP £, INR ₹, CAD , JPY ¥).
  2. Your base currency applies across all dashboards, budget limits, and reports. You can update this later in Settings > Currency.

Step 2: Select Your Account Mode

MoneyFlowTracker accommodates your preferred privacy level:

  • Guest Mode (Free & Offline): No sign-in required. Your data is stored exclusively on your device.
  • Google Account (Free): Sign in with your Google account to enable automated, private backups to your personal Google Drive app space.
  • Elite / Premium: Unlocks real-time cross-device cloud sync, unlimited AI queries, advanced analytics, and custom app icons.

Step 3: Grant Permissions (Optional but Recommended)

  • Notifications: Required to deliver daily evening summaries, budget alerts, and loan due reminders.
  • SMS (Optional): Enables automatic transaction detection from your incoming bank alerts.

3. Managing Accounts & Balances

Accounts represent the physical or digital containers holding your money.

Account Types Supported

  • Cash / Wallet: Physical cash on hand.
  • Bank Accounts: Savings and checking accounts.
  • Credit Cards: Tracks borrowed limits, card payments, and outstanding balances.
  • Digital Wallets: PayPal, Apple Cash, Google Pay balance, Paytm, etc.
  • Investment Accounts: Brokerages and mutual fund portfolios.

How to Add or Edit an Account

  1. From the bottom navigation, tap Menu and select Accounts.
  2. Tap the floating + Add Account button.
  3. Enter the Account Name (e.g., "Chase Checking", "Cash Wallet").
  4. Enter the Initial Balance.
  5. Select the Account Type and choose a visual color and icon.
  6. Tap Save. Your total net worth recalculates automatically on the Home screen.
Tip: When paying credit card bills, use the Transfer feature to move money from your Bank Account to your Credit Card. This prevents double-counting expenses!

4. Recording Transactions (Expenses, Income & Transfers)

Logging your money flow is fast, flexible, and intuitive.

Manual Transaction Entry

  1. Tap the primary + (Add) button in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Choose the transaction type:

- Expense: Money going out (e.g., Groceries, Rent, Dining).

- Income: Money coming in (e.g., Salary, Freelancing, Dividends).

- Transfer: Moving funds between your accounts (e.g., ATM Cash Withdrawal).

  1. Enter the Amount using the built-in numeric keypad.
  2. Select the appropriate Category and the source Account.
  3. (Optional) Add a Note, Merchant/Payee Name, or attach a photo of your receipt.
  4. Tap Save Transaction.

5. Automated Bank Transaction Detection

Tired of manually entering every cup of coffee? MoneyFlowTracker can automatically detect transactions from banking alerts!

How Automated Detection Works

  1. When your bank sends an SMS or notification (e.g., "Debited INR 450.00 at Starbucks on Card ending 1234"), MoneyFlowTracker securely parses the message.
  2. The app never uploads your messages to third-party servers. Matching is done locally using rule-based recognition patterns.
  3. A non-intrusive notification appears: "Detected: $4.50 at Starbucks. Confirm?"
  4. With one tap on Confirm, the transaction is permanently recorded and your account balance is updated.

Reviewing Detected Transactions

  • Open Menu > Detected Transactions to view all staged incoming transactions.
  • You can approve, edit categories, or dismiss unrecognized alerts in bulk.
Privacy Guarantee: MoneyFlowTracker never reads personal OTPs, private chats, or non-financial messages. The parser activates strictly on financial keywords.

6. Smart Receipt Scanning (OCR) & Voice Input

Camera Receipt Scanner (OCR)

  1. On the Add Transaction screen, tap the Camera / Scan icon.
  2. Snap a photo of your physical store or restaurant receipt (or select from your photo gallery).
  3. The on-device optical character recognition automatically reads the Total Amount, Date, and Merchant Name, pre-populating your form instantly.

Voice Input (Speech-to-Text)

  1. Tap the Microphone icon on the Add screen or use the 1x1 Voice Add Widget on your home screen.
  2. Speak naturally: "Spent 25 dollars on lunch at Subway using Cash."
  3. The AI engine parses your words into the correct amount, category, and account!

7. Splitting Bills & Group Expenses

Sharing expenses with roommates, partners, or travel companions is effortless.

How to Split an Expense

  1. Open any recorded transaction or tap Add > Split Transaction.
  2. Select an existing Group (e.g., "Apartment 4B", "Road Trip 2026") or create a new group.
  3. Choose how to divide the cost:

- Equally: Divides the total bill evenly among selected members.

- Custom Amounts: Specify exact contributions per person.

  1. The app tracks who owes whom and calculates settlement balances without confusing math.

8. Budgets & Spending Limits

Take control of impulsive spending by setting proactive monthly targets.

Setting Up a Category Budget

  1. Navigate to Budgets from the bottom navigation or menu.
  2. Tap Add Budget and select a category (e.g., "Dining Out", "Shopping", "Entertainment").
  3. Enter your Monthly Spending Limit (e.g., $300 / month).
  4. Save the budget.

Tracking Budget Progress

  • Green Progress Bar (< 75%): You are safely within your budget.
  • Amber Progress Bar (75% – 90%): Approaching the spending limit.
  • Red Alert (> 100%): Over budget! MoneyFlowTracker delivers a high-priority alert when a transaction pushes you past 100%.

12. Financial Health & Freedom Simulator

MoneyFlowTracker transforms raw numbers into actionable financial intelligence.

Financial Health Score (0 to 100)

Located directly on your Home screen, your Health Score evaluates four critical pillars:

  1. Savings Rate (30%): How much surplus income you retain each month.
  2. Budget Discipline (30%): How consistently you stay within category limits.
  3. Emergency Buffer (20%): Progress toward having 3 to 6 months of living expenses saved.
  4. Debt-to-Income (20%): The proportion of your earnings allocated to debt payments.

Financial Freedom Progress (FFP)

Measures your journey toward financial independence:

\text{FFP Score} = \left( \frac{\text{Monthly Passive Income}}{\text{Average Monthly Expenses}} \right) \times 100

When your score reaches 100%, your passive income completely covers your living expenses!

Future Simulator

Project your wealth 5, 10, or 25 years into the future using compound interest projections based on your real savings rate.

16. Security, Privacy & Biometric Lock

Your financial peace of mind is our highest engineering priority.

Biometric App Lock

  1. Open Settings > Security & Privacy.
  2. Toggle on Biometric Lock.
  3. Choose your preferred authentication method: Fingerprint, Face Unlock, or Device PIN/Pattern.
  4. Configure the auto-lock timer (Immediate, 1 Minute, or 5 Minutes).

Hardware-Backed Data Encryption

All records stored on your phone are continuously protected with 256-bit AES SQLCipher encryption, linked to your device's hardware Android Keystore. Even if someone gains physical access to your phone's file storage, your database cannot be read without the cryptographic key.

17. Backups, Cloud Sync & Data Export

Automated Google Drive Backups

  1. Navigate to Profile > Backup & Restore.
  2. Tap Sign in with Google.
  3. Toggle on Automatic Daily Backups.
  4. Backups are stored in your private Google Drive AppData folder, ensuring total isolation from other files.

Manual Local Backup (.db / JSON)

  • Create an encrypted snapshot at any time and save it to your phone storage, SD card, or email it to yourself.

Exporting Reports (PDF & Excel / CSV)

  1. Go to Transactions or Financial Overview.
  2. Tap the Export icon.
  3. Select your date range and file format (Excel .xlsx, CSV, or Formatted PDF Report).
  4. Tap Generate & Share to send the report via WhatsApp, email, or Google Drive.

18. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Troubleshooting

Q1: Is MoneyFlowTracker free to use?

Yes. All core tracking features, offline encryption, manual backups, category budgets, loans, and standard reports are completely free. Optional Elite subscriptions unlock real-time multi-device cloud sync and unlimited AI prompts.

Q2: Can the app access my bank accounts or money?

No. MoneyFlowTracker is read-only for incoming notification and SMS alerts. It has zero capability to move money, initiate transfers, or access your bank credentials.

Q3: What happens if I switch to a new phone?

  • If using Google Drive: Simply install MoneyFlowTracker on your new device, sign in with your Google account, and tap Restore from Drive.
  • If using Local Backups: Copy your .db backup file to your new device and select Restore from File.

Q4: Why are bank SMS not detecting automatically?

  1. Ensure the SMS permission is granted in Settings > Permissions.
  2. Verify your bank's sender ID is not marked as spam in your default SMS app.
  3. If using notification tracking, ensure Notification Listener access is enabled in Android System Settings.
